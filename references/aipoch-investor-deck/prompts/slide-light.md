# Light Canvas slide prompt

```text
Using the AIPOCH investor-deck preset, compose one 16:9 Light Canvas slide.
ROLE: [slide type and narrative job]
THESIS: [one sentence]
EVIDENCE: [up to three proof points with dated sources]
LAYOUT: 12-column grid, minimum 5% safe margin, fixed title baseline, source lower left, folio lower right.
BRAND: content-page running label only; do not repeat the complete logo.
CANVAS: #FCFCFA or #F7F7F4 with #111111 text, #6B6B66 secondary text, #E7E5DE structure, and one #FBDD67 focus.
TYPE: Noto Sans SC for Chinese, Inter for English, Roboto Mono for labels and sources; obey Investor Deck minimum sizes.
EXCLUDE: card shadows, glow, gradient, pill controls, dense decoration, unsourced claims, and illustration unless the slide role requires it.
```
