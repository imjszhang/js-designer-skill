# Four-act investor narrative · 22 slide roles

This is a reusable narrative skeleton, not a copy of any audited deck. Replace
every bracketed claim with approved company evidence. Do not import names,
financing figures, internal IDs, or management assertions from r5/r6.

Target rhythm: five black pages (about 23%) and 6–8 illustration opportunities.

## Act I — Why the system must change

1. **Cover · Black Hero · illustrated**  
   Company/category thesis, one-line promise, date, Level 1 Inverse logo.
2. **Category shift · Light Statement**  
   The external change that creates urgency; one dated proof.
3. **Broken workflow · Light Problem Cards**  
   Three observable pains across the current research journey.
4. **Cost of fragmentation · Light Metric Grid**  
   Quantified impact with units, periods, and public or approved sources.
5. **Strategic premise · Black System Frame**  
   One system-level insight that reframes the problem.

## Act II — What is being built and why it wins

6. **Product chapter · Black Hero · illustrated**  
   Product idea in one sentence; Level 2 Inverse logo.
7. **Product experience · Light Product Screenshot Split**  
   Real approved UI and three verified user-value callouts.
8. **Workflow · Light Process · illustrated optional**  
   Three to five stages from intent to validated output.
9. **System architecture · Light Architecture Layers**  
   Product, intelligence, data, and trust layers at an investor-readable level.
10. **Defensibility · Light System Map**  
    Data, workflow, evaluation, and network advantages with evidence.
11. **Proof of product · Light Metric Grid**  
    Adoption, quality, speed, or retention metrics clearly marked as actual.

## Act III — How the network and business compound

12. **Community chapter · Black Split · illustrated**  
    Community/network thesis, one proof point, Level 2 Inverse logo.
13. **Research ecosystem · Light Community Network · illustrated**  
    Actors, institutions, and value exchange with up to three facts.
14. **Activity to value · Light Process · illustrated optional**  
    How participation produces reusable knowledge or outcomes.
15. **Go-to-market · Light Flywheel · illustrated**  
    Five or six causal stages with one active path.
16. **Market framing · Light Metric Grid**  
    Category sizing with methodology and date; no unsupported precision.
17. **Competition · Light Comparison Matrix**  
    Public factual comparison on consistently defined criteria.
18. **Commercial turn · Black Statement**  
    The one reason value capture improves as the system scales.
19. **Business model · Light Revenue Bridge**  
    Revenue units, key drivers, and explicit actual/forecast/target separation.

## Act IV — Why this team and why now

20. **Execution plan · Light Roadmap**  
    Three horizons, six milestones maximum, commitments separated from options.
21. **Team and raise · Light Team / Use of Funds Split**  
    Role-market fit plus approved raise/use/milestone logic; keep readable.
22. **Close · Black Hero · illustrated optional**  
    One investable thesis, contact, and Level 1 Inverse or Stacked logo.

## Editing rules

- The 22 roles are defaults, not a requirement to preserve weak or duplicate
  content. Combine only when font and evidence minimums still hold.
- Each slide advances one question. Put secondary detail in speaker notes or an
  appendix.
- Use illustration on narrative/system pages; use real product screenshots on
  product pages and native charts/tables on analytical pages.
- Avoid consecutive illustration-only pages. Alternate thesis, evidence, and
  mechanism.
- A black page is narrative punctuation, never a way to hide dense content.
- Add appendix slides for detailed market methods, security, technical
  architecture, unit economics, or source registers when required.
